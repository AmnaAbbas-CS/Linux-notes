# Episode 1: Navigating the Terminal

## What I learned

| Command | What it does | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|
| `pwd` | Shows where I am | Avoids changing or deleting the wrong place |
| `ls` | Lists files and folders | Finding files is the first step in any investigation |
| `cd` | Changes folder | Moves around the system |
| `cd ..` | Goes up one folder | Backs out of deep folders |

## Notes
- My home folder is `/home/amna`. `/` is the top of the system.
- Linux is **case-sensitive**: `home` and `Home` are different names.
- `cd ..` needs a space. `cd..` does not work.
- `cd ~` jumps to my home folder from anywhere.
